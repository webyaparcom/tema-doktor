How it works

From appointment to follow-up

The clinic visit in four steps.

  1. 01

    Appointment

    Choose a day and time by phone, WhatsApp or the form.

  2. 02

    Registration

    Your ID and a note on your complaint are recorded.

  3. 03

    Examination

    The doctor listens, examines you and requests tests if needed.

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    Results are discussed; a plan and follow-up date are set.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions and short answers.

  1. help

    Can I come without an appointment?

    Patients with appointments are seen first; walk-ins are accepted as availability allows. We suggest calling first.

  2. help

    When are test results ready?

    It depends on the test; an estimated time is given when the sample is taken.

  3. help

    What should I do in an emergency?

    Call 112 first. The clinic is not a substitute for an emergency room.

  4. help

    Are the images and data on the site real?

    No; this is a setup example. Images are representative and names, figures and people are fictional. Nothing here is medical advice.
